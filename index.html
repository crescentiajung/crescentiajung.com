About

Hello! I'm Crescentia, an Information Science PhD Candidate at Cornell University on the Cornell Tech campus in NYC. I am advised by Professor Shiri Azenkot.

I research and design technology to support inclusive virtual collaboration. My work contributes to Human-Computer Interaction, Computer-Supported Cooperative Work, Human-AI Interaction, and Accessibility.

Before Cornell, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. There I worked with Professor Yea-Seul Kim on accessible visualization research.

Feel free to reach out at cj382@cornell.edu!
