About

Hello! I'm Crescentia, an Information Science PhD Candidate at Cornell University on the Cornell Tech campus in NYC. I am advised by Professor Shiri Azenkot (opens in new tab).

I research and design AI-powered systems that support inclusive collaboration in mixed-ability teams. My work contributes to Human-Computer Interaction, Computer-Supported Cooperative Work, Human-AI Interaction, and Accessibility.

I am also a Siegel and Rubinstein PiTech PhD Impact Fellow with the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, where I evaluate how AI chatbots answer public health questions.

Before Cornell, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. There I worked with Professor Yea-Seul Kim (opens in new tab) on accessible visualization research.

Feel free to reach out at cj382@cornell.edu!
